How it grew

It started with one question.

“Did the kids get home?” HomeCircle began as a family map that’s easy on the battery and tells no one but the family. Then families asked it to do more, one everyday moment at a time.

  1. 1

    Where is everyone?

    A live family map, arrival alerts for Home and School, and Locate for a fresh fix when you need one.

  2. 2

    Are you OK?

  3. 3

Everything a family shares, in one private place

Each circle’s admins choose which of these are on. Keep it to a map, or use all of it.

Where is everyone

  • Live family map with battery and “last seen”, updated live while the app is open.
  • Locate wakes a phone for one fresh GPS fix. No constant tracking needed.
  • Place alerts: “Jo arrived at School.” Geofences run on each phone, so the server never learns where your places are.
  • Places your way: long-press any place to change its name, spot or size. Most visited places get real names, like “1.2 mi north of Home”.
  • Your battery, your call: a slider from longest battery to most up to date. It goes quiet while you’re still, catches up while charging, and your circle sees how often you update.

Are you OK?

  • Check-ins: “Where are you?” with a one-tap “I’m OK”.
  • SOS: hold to alert everyone, share your location every minute, and see safe places nearby.
  • Low-battery handoff: a last location at 10% and 5%.
  • Chirps: a bird sound or a flash on someone’s phone, quiet during school hours.

Talk it over

  • Group chat and direct messages in one list. A direct message can be read only by the two of you.
  • Calls from anyone’s card or chat: in HomeCircle, or on WhatsApp.
  • Polls with live bars, emoji options, deadlines and a wheel for ties.
  • Read ticks, which each person can turn off.

Your circle, your rules

  • Choose the features: turn off SOS, chirps, polls or Locate and they disappear from everyone’s phone.
  • Several circles, one server: your household, your parents and siblings, each with its own key.
  • Pause sharing anytime, and decide how long messages are kept.

Calls

Call each other, right from the map.

Tap someone and tap Call. Pick how: a private call in HomeCircle, or the WhatsApp everyone already has.

  • Calls in HomeCircle: voice goes phone to phone, end-to-end encrypted, only the two of you. Answer from the lock screen, even when the app is closed.
  • Or on WhatsApp: the same Call button opens WhatsApp with their number, for anyone who prefers it.
  • One-tap group calls: tap Group call in the family chat and everyone gets a notification. One tap on it and they’re in the circle’s WhatsApp call.
  • In the chat: missed calls, how long you talked, and who joined the group call.

An admin turns calls on for the circle.

For grandparents

Made for Nani, too.

Switch a grandparent’s phone to a screen made for her: the family’s faces on big tiles, one button to ask for a call, and SOS. Nothing else to get lost in.

  • Call her like anyone else: her phone rings with a screen made for her. It says who’s calling in Urdu, has one big button to answer, and puts the sound on the speaker.
  • Call me back: she taps one button, or one person’s photo, to ask the family to call. She hears your answer in Urdu.
  • Photos on her tiles, so she knows who’s who at a glance.
  • Quiet while she sleeps: her sleep hours stay quiet, and her SOS always rings through. If her phone goes silent for too long, the family is asked to check on her.
  • Set up from your phone: an admin switches anyone between the full app, the elder screen and the grandkids screen, from anywhere.

Optional · off until you add it

It remembers, so you don’t have to.

Add a helper to your circle and give it a name, like Birdie. Send it the school calendar, a recipe, a receipt or a question, and it keeps what matters for everyone.

  • Calendars: school and sports calendars from a link, a PDF or a photo, checked for changes. “When are the kids free for 5 days?” gets a real answer.
  • Recipes, kept whole: every ingredient and step, and it can put what you need on a shopping list.
  • Contacts, documents, trips and a home manual: the dentist and the plumber, passports with a reminder before they expire, the next trip, the filter to change every 3 months.
  • Health notes, private to you unless you say they’re for the circle.
  • Shopping lists per store, with everything you’ve bought there ready to pick, and estimated prices and a cost range from your receipts.
  • Long-term reminders: car registration, insurance, refills, to every admin until someone taps Done.

You stay in charge of it

  • Your choice of AI: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral or a model on your own machine, with your own key.
  • A monthly budget you set, paid to the provider directly.
  • Names masked: your family becomes Person A and Place 1 before anything reaches the AI.
  • It can’t read your chats. It only sees what someone sends it.
  • It never keeps secrets: no passwords, codes, or whole card or ID numbers, only the last 4 digits. It tells you when it leaves one out.
  • Memory you can see and keep: search, edit, delete and undo every note, and download it all any time as notes you can open in Obsidian.

Air quality and pollen for trees, grass and weeds, wherever they are.

WhatsApp

Everyone’s location in your Home Assistant, for “lights on when Maya gets home”. Each person can turn theirs off.

Private by design, not by promise

Most family apps keep every family’s data on their own servers. HomeCircle flips that: you own the server, and it still can’t read your data.

  • Encrypted on your phone. Locations, names, messages and places are sealed with a key only your circle holds (XSalsa20-Poly1305 + Ed25519 signatures).
  • Your server sees ciphertext. It stores and relays sealed data, never plaintext. Your hosting provider sees the same.
  • Calls stay between phones. Voice in a HomeCircle call goes phone to phone, encrypted.
  • The helper is your choice. It’s a member of your circle that reads only what you send it, and keeps its memory encrypted with its own key.
  • Only your server’s members. There is no global network and no way to find or add people outside your server.
  • Content-free notifications. Push messages say only “new activity”. Your phone decrypts the details itself.
  • No history on the server. Only each person’s latest location is kept. Old messages expire after 90 days by default.

Up and running in about ten minutes

  1. 1

    Pick a server

    Deploy HomeCircle with one click on Render or Railway, rent a small VPS, or run it at home on a Raspberry Pi or as a Home Assistant app. One server can hold several circles, each kept separate.

  2. 2

    Set up in the app

    Open the HomeCircle app, enter your server address and its one-time setup code. Your encryption keys are created on your phone.

  3. 3

    Invite your family

    Show a QR code or send a private link. Only people who join your server can ever see your location or messages.

Choose where your server lives

Each option runs the same open-source server. Pick based on how hands-off you want to be.

Check your server

Once it’s deployed, paste its address to make sure it’s reachable and ready for setup.

Get the app

Install the Android app on everyone’s phone, then choose Set up a new server (first person) or Join my family (everyone else).

On a laptop? Every server also has a web version: just open your server’s address in a browser.

Questions

Who can see my location?

Only people who joined your HomeCircle server with an invite. The server stores your location encrypted, and only the members’ phones hold the key.

Does it drain my battery?

No more than you choose. By default your phone updates about every 3 minutes while you’re moving and every 15 when you’re still, using at most about 3% of the battery a day. Slide toward Longest battery to save more, or Most up to date for more detail. Arrivals, Locate and SOS always go through.

What does the helper see, and what does it cost?

Only what someone sends it: a question with its name, a file, or a message you choose to Remember. It can’t read your chats. Questions go to the AI provider your admin picked, with names swapped out, and your circle pays that provider directly, within a monthly budget you set.

Can my parents use it?

Yes. An admin can switch a grandparent’s phone to a simple screen: big photo tiles, one button to ask for a call back, and SOS. Family can call her, and her phone tells her who’s calling in Urdu.

Who can hear our calls?

Only the people on the call. A HomeCircle call goes phone to phone, encrypted. Calls on WhatsApp are WhatsApp’s, as usual.

Do we have to use the helper?

No. It’s off until an admin adds it, and the map, chat, SOS and plugins work without it.

What can my hosting provider see?

Encrypted data, how many people use the server, and when phones connect. They can’t see names, locations, places or messages.

What if I lose my phone?

Another admin removes the old phone from Settings, which changes the circle’s key. Then they invite you again on your new phone. Tip: make a second family member an admin.

What does it cost?

The software is free. Hosting costs roughly $3–7 a month, or nothing extra on a Raspberry Pi or Home Assistant you already own.

Can people on other servers find me?

No. There’s no directory and no federation. Each server is its own closed circle.
